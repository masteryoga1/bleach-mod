# Bleach

A Bleach mod for Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge. The goal is to stay as close to Tite Kubo's manga and anime as a Minecraft mod can: become a Shinigami, a Quincy or a Hollow, fight Hollows, and travel to Soul Society and Hueco Mundo.

[Bleach KD](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/bleach_kd) (KnightDemon, continuing LittleBreadLoaf's 1.12.2 mod) is used as a design reference only. No code or art is copied from it; the art in this repo is placeholder for now.

The roadmap lives in [docs/FEATURES.md](docs/FEATURES.md).

## Status

Step 2 is done, step 3, the Shinigami path, is playable from the Academy to Shikai, and step 4 is under way: becoming a Quincy is in, and so are step 4b, the Quincy arts, and step 4c, Quincy in the world. The Hollows and Arrancar round has begun: players can now live as Hollows, rise through the Menos Grande to Vasto Lorde and tear off their masks to become Arrancar with Sonído, Hierro, Pesquisa and Bala, and Menos Grande mobs and Arrancar raiders have come. The Visored round is in: a Shinigami can take a Hollow into its wounds, which may seize its body near death, beat it in a fight, then wear its mask, fire a Cero from it and turn its Getsuga Tenshō black. The Kidō round is in: Shinigami, Substitutes and Academy students cast Hadō and Bakudō by name or by saying their incantations in chat, heal with Kaidō and choose their spells on the soul screen's Kidō page, and the Kidō stat trains, is taught to students once a day by the Academy instructor and is examined. Soul Society's round has begun: its land is in, and a Shinigami can open a Senkaimon to reach it. A Senkaimon takes a soul without a guide through the Dangai. Hueco Mundo's land is in too, reached through a Garganta that a Menos or an Arrancar tears open. So far:

- Every player has a soul: a soul state (starting as a living Human), a reiryoku pool that regenerates (twice as fast while meditating or sleeping), and the Zankensoki stats, which level up by use.
- Reiryoku grows by beating Hollows (more for purifying them with a zanpakutō), performing Konsō, surviving close calls (including a Totem of Undying save) and levelling stats.
- A spiritually aware Human who dies comes back where they fell as a Plus, with a Chain of Fate hanging from their chest. The chain wears away over an hour of play. In its last quarter Encroachment sets in, with pangs of pain and blindness, and when it runs out the player becomes a Hollow, with a bone mask, a hole through the chest and a burst of reiryoku. A Konsō from a Shinigami saves a Plus. The chain wears only in the Human World and the Dangai between the worlds, and holds elsewhere unless the Plus gnaws at it. In the Overworld a Shinigami on duty comes to one who finds none.
- Players below the awareness threshold (30 maximum reiryoku by default) cannot see Pluses, Hollows or other spirits, and their hits, arrows and potions pass straight through them. Just below the threshold, a faint shimmer and distant screams give hidden Hollows away.
- Reiatsu difference: hits from someone with far less spiritual pressure than you do much less damage.
- Press **R** to release your reiatsu. It drains reiryoku and weighs down much weaker creatures and players nearby with Slowness and Mining Fatigue (and Weakness for the far weaker), and creatures far below you stop attacking and run. Running dry ends it and exhausts you. It spares your mount, your pets and players you can't hurt (PvP off, or a team without friendly fire).
- A reiryoku bar beside the hotbar (outlined while reiatsu is released, with a chain gauge beside it for a Plus, a hunger gauge for a Hollow and a mask gauge for a Visored), and a soul screen on **K**, with a Kidō page for those who can use Kidō. Every key can be rebound under the Bleach category.
- The Shin'ō Academy: a small hall of white plaster, shōji and dark roof tiles in Seireitei, at (-192, 224) in Soul Society; its shōji, door and tatami floor burn. A Plus senses that it lies in Soul Society, and there which way, by the nearest of Seireitei's gates from outside its wall. Its instructor enrolls souls that have passed on and hands them an Asauchi, an unbreakable sword that awakens into their own zanpakutō. Once a day the instructor gives a student a Kidō lesson: 40 Kidō xp, and the next spell to learn, with its incantation (with chat casting on). Academies built in the Overworld before keep their instructors, who send a Plus on to Soul Society.
- Graduation: a student with level 5 Zanjutsu, Hakuda, Hohō and Kidō becomes a Shinigami, with 25 more reiryoku and a Shihakushō.
- The Substitute route: a wounded Shinigami sometimes turns up at night near an aware Human, and can give them its power. A Shinigami player can do the same by sneaking and using their blade on a Human twice; when the Human accepts by using an empty hand on them, half the Shinigami's reiryoku is lent for 20 minutes. The Human stays alive as a Substitute Shinigami and becomes a full Shinigami if they die.
- Konsō: sneak and use your blade on a Plus player, who accepts by using an empty hand on you. They pass on and wake in a district of the Rukongai in Soul Society, drawn by chance with the inner ones likelier, and are told its name and the way to the Academy; you gain reiryoku. In the Overworld, once a Plus's chain has worn five minutes, a Shinigami on duty comes down nearby to offer it Konsō, returns to Soul Society three minutes later, and comes again every ten minutes while the Plus refuses. One comes too to a soul that has passed on and is found in the Overworld with no bed or anchor of its own there, and sends it home to Soul Society if it stays near for half a minute. Plus spirits are sent on the same way, without asking. With no bed or anchor of its own, a soul that has passed on wakes at its Rukongai home when it dies (at the Academy, where the server sends souls straight there), a student at the Academy, a Shinigami at the Senkaimon court, and a Gillian, Adjuchas or Vasto Lorde Hollow or an Arrancar in Hueco Mundo, outside where Las Noches will stand, unless a purifying blow freed it.
- Hollows come through Garganta tears in the sky near spiritually aware souls, day or night, more often the stronger your reiatsu: Spider Hollows that climb walls, Hound Hollows in packs, Serpent Hollows and the Plus-hunting Hexapodus.
- Strike a Hollow's mask for double damage. Killing one with a zanpakutō purifies it and sends its soul on.
- Your first Hollow is Fishbone D, and a wounded Shinigami may arrive just ahead of it, offering her power.
- Pluses wander the world with their chains, and villagers who die in the Overworld leave one behind. Leave a Plus alone too long and it becomes a Hollow.
- As a Hollow, soul hunger replaces food: hold **J** (Devour) on a weakened soul to seize and devour it, for hunger, reiryoku, health and souls. Your claws and hide grow with your reiryoku, you heal fast while fed, see in the dark (Sneak and **J** turns it off) and cannot wear armour. Hollows come for you through Garganta. A Shinigami's zanpakutō, or kneeling before the Academy's instructor, cleanses you back into a soul.
- Devour enough as a Hollow and you rise through the Menos Grande: a towering, mindless **Gillian**, then an **Adjuchas** with its will back that must keep eating Hollows or sink back, and at last a human-sized **Vasto Lorde**, of which a server holds only a handful. From Gillian up, hold **Y** (Cero) to gather a Cero at your mouth and let go to fire it. A Gillian can press **G** while looking at a hurt comrade to call down Negación, a pillar of light that holds them untouchable and lifts them away.
- An Adjuchas or Vasto Lorde can press **Z** twice to tear off its mask. It may fail, though a Vasto Lorde nearly never does. An Arrancar keeps a fragment of its mask and its hole, eats and wears armour again, keeps its Cero, and carries its Hollow power sealed in its own Spanish-named blade. With the blade in hand, **Z** calls out Resurrección, which frees and heals its Hollow form for as long as its reiryoku lasts, and **B** seals it again. An Arrancar has arts of its own: **V** is Sonído, a flash step that needs no training; **G** fires a Bala, a quick, weak crimson shot (hold it to keep firing); holding **J** still for a second sends out Pesquisa, which outlines every soul nearby through walls (though not through Sekkiseki) and tells you where the strongest is; its Hierro skin turns aside part of every blow from anyone not far stronger; and an Espada can press **Sneak + Y** to spill its blood into a Gran Rey Cero, a far greater Cero in its own colour.
- Every Arrancar is a Número, numbered from 11 in the order it is born on the server. The ten strongest with at least 1,500 maximum reiryoku hold the seats of the Espada, the strongest the Primera, with their number tattooed somewhere on the body; one that loses its seat to a stronger Arrancar, or by staying away a week, becomes a Privaron Espada until it wins one back. The white Arrancar uniform (a jacket with a black sash, a hakama, and zōri with black tabi) is sewn from wool at the Tailoring Table. Each Espada carries a Caja Negación: used from behind on a Hollow or an Arrancar within reach, it shuts them away unharmed, where no one can touch or see them, for up to a minute (a fellow Espada for 5 seconds), once every 5 minutes.
- At night, strong souls may draw Arrancar through a Garganta: a lone Número, or a Privaron Espada with two to four Números as its Fracción, who follow it, fight first and guard it. They fight with the blade, Sonído, Bala, Cero and Hierro, leave after a while, and a beaten Privaron Espada may be lifted away with its Fracción by a Negación. They leave Hollows and Arrancar alone and never hunt a seated Espada. A seated Espada that beats a Número with no living leader sees it kneel: use it with an empty hand within 30 seconds to take it as your Fracción, up to three. Your Fracción follow you, fight whatever strikes you and whatever you strike, never leave, never despawn and wait where they are while you are away; Sneak and use one twice to send it off.
- A Shinigami or Substitute with a named zanpakutō and at least 500 maximum reiryoku can become a **Visored**. Three near-fatal blows from Menos-class Hollows (two from any Hollow for a Substitute), at least 5 minutes apart, plant a Hollow in its soul, and it talks. A Minecraft day later, sitting in Jinzen brings the inner Hollow out to fight in a ring for up to 3 minutes. Losing never kills, and the fight can be tried again later. Beating it makes the soul a Visored, who can press **M** to call its Hollow mask: faster, stronger and heavier with reiatsu, for seconds at first and longer the more it is worn, up to 3 minutes. Villagers shun a masked Visored and Pluses flee it. With its mask on, a Visored's Getsuga Tenshō turns black with a red edge and hits half again as hard, and after 10 minutes of masks worn it also fires a crimson Cero with **Y** that poisons a Quincy it hits. Until it is beaten, the Hollow may seize the body when a blow leaves it at a fifth of its health or less: half a mask forms, and for 10 seconds the soul hits harder, heals and no blow takes it below one heart, but its Shikai, Getsuga Tenshō, Shunpo and Konsō do not answer; **M** tears the half mask off. A masked Visored who looses a black Getsuga Tenshō at a quarter of its health or less loses control the same way for 5 seconds. A server can let a third lost fight turn the soul into a Hollow.
- A Plus can gnaw its own Chain of Fate by holding **J**: a quick way to become a Hollow.
- Shinigami carry a Denreishinki, the soul pager, which beeps when Hollows arrive nearby.
- Press **V** to flash step (Shunpo) from Hohō 5: a few blocks in an instant, further, cheaper and more often as Hohō grows.
- Reishi footing from Hohō 3: jump again while falling (or press the Reishi Footing key, unbound at first) to stand on hardened reishi in mid-air, and sneak to sink through it. It drains reiryoku while you stand.
- The Shihakushō (kosode, hakama, and waraji with tabi), made from wool and wheat at a Tailoring Table. It gives no armour, but the full set speeds up reiryoku regeneration for Shinigami, Substitutes and students. A zanpakutō in your hotbar is drawn sheathed at your hip.
- Bond with your Asauchi by carrying it, meditating, sleeping, surviving close calls and purifying Hollows with it. It whispers as the bond grows.
- Jinzen: sneak and hold use with your blade on solid ground to meet its spirit, and pass its test to learn its name. How you fought while bonding decides which spirit you meet (Zangetsu or Zabimaru for now; spirits are datapack files).
- Press **Z** to release Shikai and **B** to seal it. Shikai drains reiryoku. Zabimaru lashes out several blocks; Zangetsu never seals once released.
- Hold **G** with Zangetsu released to gather a Getsuga Tenshō and let go to swing it: a piercing crescent that purifies Hollows and cuts soft blocks where mob griefing allows.
- Press **U** to cast Kidō by name, and sneak and press it to choose the next spell, or pick any you know on the soul screen's Kidō page. Hadō #1 Shō pushes, and #4 Byakurai (from Kidō 3) is a narrow bolt through two creatures. Each cast costs reiryoku and trains Kidō, and Kidō waits while a Getsuga Tenshō or a Cero gathers. Hadō #31 Shakkahō (from Kidō 5) and #33 Sōkatsui (from Kidō 8) have incantations: type one whole in chat, and it is spoken aloud to those nearby who see spirits and the spell goes off at full strength a moment later, unless a blow breaks the chant. By name with the key they need Kidō 10 and 14, are weaker, and may burst in your hand until you master them. Sneak and press **U** on a spell to see its incantation in chat. Hadō #58 Tenran (from Kidō 18) is a gust that widens ahead of you, lightly hurting and throwing back everything in it and blinding the players it reaches for a moment; it is weaker without a zanpakutō in hand or on your hotbar. Hadō #63 Raikōhō is chanted from Kidō 25, or cast by name from 32 by holding **U** while a yellow orb gathers in your palm for a second: let go sooner and it scatters, and a blow breaks it. Bakudō #1 Sai (from Kidō 1) binds the arms of the first creature in your sight within 8 blocks behind its back for up to 3 seconds: it cannot strike, use items or cast. Bakudō #4 Hainawa (from Kidō 4) throws a yellow rope up to 12 blocks that holds a creature where it stands. Bakudō #9 Geki (chanted from Kidō 7, by name from 12) binds the arms and holds the body of the first creature in your sight within 10 blocks for up to 2 seconds, in a red glow, and #61 Rikujōkōrō (chanted from Kidō 22, by name from 28) does both for up to 5 seconds, in six rods of light. A body its Hollow has seized, held for 3 seconds, is pinned, and the seizure ends. A stronger soul is held for less, and shrugs off a bind less than half its strength. Bakudō #21 Sekienton (from Kidō 8) leaves a cloud of red smoke where you stand for 4 seconds: creatures in it lose you, players in it are blinded, and you run faster for 2 seconds. Bakudō #39 Enkōsen (from Kidō 12) raises a round shield before you for 3 seconds that takes the blows from the front up to its strength, and breaks under a bigger one, which lands only what is left of it. Bakudō #81 Dankū (from Kidō 36) raises a pale wall of light 5 blocks wide and 4 tall two blocks before you for 6 seconds: bodies and binds pass through it, but every Hadō and every arrow stops at it, and so does a Cero, Bala, Gran Rey Cero, Getsuga Tenshō, Heilig Pfeil or Letzt Pfeil unless its shooter's reiatsu (halved for a Bala or a Heilig Pfeil, doubled for a Gran Rey Cero) is more than 1¼ times yours at the cast, when the wall shatters and the shot goes on. Kaidō (from Kidō 3) heals while you hold **U**: the first creature in your sight within about 3 blocks, its reiryoku first if it is below half, then its health, further and faster as your Kidō grows (to half health from Kidō 3, three quarters from 12 with Poison lifted, full from 25, and from 40 Wither lifted too); it never lifts Slowness, Weakness, Mining Fatigue or Nausea. With no one in reach, from Kidō 40 you heal yourself at half the rate. Each second of Kaidō that heals, lifts Poison or Wither, or refills costs 2% of your reiryoku and trains Kidō, and your reiryoku does not refill while you hold **U**, or for up to a second after.
- Hold **O** with your own zanpakutō or a nameless Asauchi in hand to turn it like a key: after 1½ seconds a Senkaimon of two lit shōji opens 2 blocks ahead for 30 seconds, for 5% of your maximum reiryoku (at least 5), and the next waits 30 seconds (creative mode never pays or waits). Letting go sooner, a blow that lands or opening a screen stops the turn. It opens only in the Human World and in Soul Society, and never into Sekkiseki. Stand in it for a second with a Jigokuchō (a Hell Butterfly) and a Shinigami's power of your own to pass: from the Human World to the Senkaimon court's square in Seireitei, and from Soul Society back to where you last left the Human World (else your bed there, else world spawn). Without a Jigokuchō, or without a Shinigami's power of your own, the gate takes you into the Dangai instead (below): a lost Jigokuchō means the Dangai, not being stuck. Mounted, or while your Hollow holds your body, you are turned away, and no Senkaimon opens right beside another. Graduates are handed a Jigokuchō, and a Shinigami never handed one, such as one from before this version, gets one at its next login. An Academy instructor hands a Shinigami or a Substitute that carries none another Jigokuchō, once a game day.
- As a Gillian, Adjuchas or Vasto Lorde Hollow, or an Arrancar, hold **O** for a second to tear at the air: a Garganta opens before you for 20 seconds, as tall as your body needs, for 5% of your maximum reiryoku (at least 5), and the next waits 30 seconds (creative mode never pays or waits). Letting go sooner, a blow that lands or opening a screen stops it. It opens only in the Human World, Soul Society and Hueco Mundo, and never right beside another gate or into Sekkiseki; a basic Hollow is told that only a Menos or an Arrancar can open one. Step in and stand there for half a second to pass, on foot, if your body fits it (a tear that opens round you takes you only once you have stepped out and in again): from the Human World or Soul Society into Hueco Mundo where you stand, or pushed out past where Las Noches will stand, and from Hueco Mundo to where you last left the Human World by a gate (else the ground where you stand, else world spawn), or, if you were sneaking as it opened, to the Rukongai's first district in Soul Society on your bearing from Hueco Mundo's centre. Mounted, or while your Hollow holds your body, you are turned away.
- New players are asked once whether Quincy blood runs in them (Human, Quincy, or let fate decide). Quincy blood is Echt or Gemischt and comes with a Quincy Cross.
- Hold use with the Cross until the Heilig Bogen forms, then draw and fire Heilig Pfeil arrows of reishi. They destroy Hollows instead of purifying them. Reishi refills by itself, and fast while meditating with the Cross, more in forests and near an open Garganta.
- From Quincy tier 2, **V** does Hirenkyaku and you can stand on reishi. **N** calls Blut Vene (defence) and Blut Arterie (attack).
- Train a week in the Sanrei Glove, handed down at tier 3, then tear it off for 90 seconds of Letzt Stil. It burns your power out; win it back through a rite of your own, or sooner with another Quincy's arrow just beside your heart.
- Won back, the power becomes Ginrei Kojaku, which streams arrows and rains Licht Regen, with Seele Schneider blades to swing, fire from the bow and plant, and later Vollständig, with wings, a halo, flight and Sklaverei.
- From tier 2, fill Gintō tubes with reishi and cast four spells from them: Gritz binds, Wolke cushions falls, Heizen (from tier 3) raises a pillar of light and Sprenger (once restored) sets off five Seele Schneider standing in a pentagon. Filled tubes work even while your power is sealed.
- **H** is the Quincy Form key: it takes off or tears the glove, calls Licht Regen mid-draw, and starts or ends Vollständig (sneak and press it for Sklaverei).
- Hollow attacks poison a Quincy, and at 100 the soul tears apart. An awakened Quincy comes back alive rather than as a Plus, and cannot take a Shinigami's power or enroll at the Academy.
- The white Quincy garb and the Wandenreich uniform, from the Tailoring Table (now with pages).
- A Quincy Elder visits those with Quincy blood: daily lessons, the Ransōtengai trial, and an arrow beside the heart that restores a sealed power.
- A young Quincy Hunter turns up at Garganta arrivals and challenges you to a Hollow-killing contest.
- Fresh mask cracks knock off fragments. With a filled Gintō and bone meal they make Hollow Bait, which draws ever stronger waves of Hollows until the sky cracks.
- Menos Grande: towering Gillian and pack-hunting Adjuchas come now and then for strong souls, and a Gillian pushes through the cracked sky at the end of a bait. Both fire a Cero from the mouth after a charge you can see coming; a fresh blow to the mask breaks it. A Gillian eats the lesser Hollows around it and pulls back into the sky when beaten; it also lifts a beaten Hollow away in a pillar of light; Adjuchas eat weaker Hollows to heal.
- Sneak and press **H** when your body is failing to move it by threads of reishi (Ransōtengai).
- Soul Society, a world of its own, reached through a Senkaimon, through a Garganta torn while sneaking in Hueco Mundo, or by an operator's command (`/execute in bleach:soul_society run tp @s 0 72 300`): Seireitei's level lawn inside the white Seireiheki, open at its four gates (a gate house in each, a great doorway under a tiled roof, shut by a great gate faced in Sekkiseki that an operator's `/bleach gate` lifts, or its giant keeper for 30 seconds once beaten in a fight, and a wicket beside it that a soul the gates bar cannot open from the Rukongai's side, and a keeper who speaks to a soul that uses him, fights a soul the gates bar that comes before it and can touch him, and anyone who strikes him, and, once beaten, lets everyone whose blow landed through his wicket for a day), Sōkyoku Hill rising sheer from its middle with birch on top, and the Rukongai's 80 districts of farmland all round, rougher further out, with crags and wilds from the 79th district outward. The Seireiheki is Sekkiseki, which undoes reiryoku: a spirit art comes undone against it, Pesquisa feels no soul through it and no gate opens into it. Over the wall the Shakonmaku, an unseen membrane, closes Seireitei in, so wall and membrane admit souls only at the four gates (or by a Senkaimon to its court): a creature, or a player in survival or adventure, that walks, rides, flies or falls across anywhere else, or digs round a shut gate, is set back on its own side, an ender pearl or chorus fruit will not carry it over and a flash step stops short; arrows, snowballs and the like stop at it and fall, a spirit art comes undone there, and Pesquisa feels no soul through the membrane either. The action bar names each district you walk into, and its region, and a gate when you stand in its gap. The Gotei 13's barracks stand inside the wall, thirteen walled compounds, each with its division's banner on its gate: the First's at the north gate's way in by the hill, the rest on a ring nearer the wall, among them the Fourth's beds, the Twelfth's lab benches and the Thirteenth's pond (in a world made before them, a compound stands only where its corner's chunk is new, and then only in its new chunks). Paved avenues lead in from each gate towards the hill and two ring roads cross them, one round the hill and one inside the ring of barracks, with lanes between blocks of lawn walled in white plaster under a course of tiles; a Senkaimon comes out on the court, a paved square with lanterns (in a world made before the streets, they lie only on ground made since). Nothing hostile spawns there. A bed there sets your spawn, but sleeping cannot skip the night.
- Seireitei's building blocks, each crafted from vanilla materials: white plaster (bone meal, sand and clay), black plaster (white plaster round coal or charcoal), and dark roof tiles (terracotta round coal or charcoal) with their stairs and slab, which a stonecutter also cuts; shōji (sticks and paper), a paper pane that joins walls and panes as a glass pane does; the shōji door (six shōji), which swings open on its hinge as a wooden door does; and tatami (a hay bale and any wool carpet), whose weave turns with the way you face as you lay it. Paper and straw burn; plaster and tile do not. The Academy and the barracks are built of them; an Academy generated before keeps its old look. The division banner (two white wool with black dye between, in a column) hangs on the side of the block you set it against, showing its division's kanji; use it where it hangs and it turns to the next of sixteen: the thirteen divisions, the Kidō Corps, the Onmitsukidō and the Zero Division. Broken or picked, it keeps its division, and it burns as wool does.
- The Dangai, the corridor between the worlds, crossed by anyone a Senkaimon does not guide: lanes walled by the Kōryū's current, which holds you a moment if you touch it. Walk to the lane's end and you come out 24 blocks over the Rukongai, or over the way home, falling slowly. Ten seconds after the first of you enters, the Kōtotsu comes down the lane behind: walk and it catches you, sprint the whole way and you get out first. If it catches you, it throws you out unhurt but 1,000 to 2,000 blocks off course.
- Hueco Mundo, a world of its own, reached through a Garganta or by an operator's command (`/execute in bleach:hueco_mundo run tp @s 0.5 96 195.5 0 0`, which sets you on its level ground looking out over the dunes; the world is made as you arrive, so the first visit takes a moment): white dunes under a black, starless midnight that never ends, the moon overhead at the phase opposite the Overworld's, lone trees of white quartz, a level white plain round the centre where Las Noches will stand, and under the crust the Forest of Menos, a cavern of white quartz trunks reached by digging, with a fall of over 50 blocks to its floor. Its white sand, white sandstone and Hueco quartz are gathered there, and smooth white sandstone and Hueco quartz bricks made from them. Water poured there boils away, a bed lets no one sleep and a charged respawn anchor blows up. Hollows roam its dunes and Gillian and Adjuchas the Forest of Menos beneath, in any light and never in Peaceful, and by default they leave Hollow players alone, who may devour them. A Hollow player's soul hunger falls at half its rate there, and an Adjuchas's clock keeps running on the dunes and in the forest.
- A Bleach advancement tab, `/bleach` admin commands, a per-world server config (`serverconfig/bleach-server.toml`) and a client config (`config/bleach-client.toml`, for turning off footing on jump and the Hollow mask's edge).

Soul Society and Hueco Mundo are worlds added by a datapack, so Minecraft calls any world with the mod experimental and warns before opening it; NeoForge stops asking once you have said yes for a world. A world made before either gains it when next opened, and a world that loses the mod drops them and opens as before (their files stay in the world's folder), except that a player who was in Soul Society or Hueco Mundo reappears in the Overworld at the same coordinates, so leave them first.

How each stat trains:

| Stat | Trained by | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Zanjutsu | landing sword hits (double on Hollows, more on the mask; not as a Hollow) | +3% sword damage per level |
| Hakuda | landing unarmed hits (double on Hollows, more on the mask) | +0.25 unarmed damage per level |
| Hohō | sprinting, flash stepping | +1% movement speed per level; Shunpo from level 5, 0.2 blocks further and cheaper per level; reishi footing from level 3 |
| Kidō | casting Kidō (one more xp when the spell strikes; twice as much chanted; one for each second of Kaidō that heals, lifts Poison or Wither, or refills; a student's daily lesson from the Academy instructor, 40) | +2% Kidō strength per level; spells unlock by level |
| Reiatsu Control | meditating (crouching still), up to a minute at a time; landing Heilig Pfeil on Hollows; Pesquisa | +2% reiryoku regeneration per level; 2% less Shikai drain per level (down to 40%); +0.1 Heilig Pfeil damage, 2% faster reishi channelling and 0.1 blocks more Hirenkyaku and 1 block more Pesquisa per level |

`tools/` holds the scripts that make the placeholder art and the structure templates. They need only Python 3, and `--check` confirms the committed files match what they make.

Zanpakutō spirits are data: a datapack adds one at `data/<namespace>/bleach/zanpakuto_spirit/<name>.json`. They are read when a world loads, not on `/reload`.

Kidō spells are data too, at `data/<namespace>/bleach/kido_spell/<name>.json`. They are read when a world loads and sent to players as they join. A spell's `incantations` are the whole lines that cast it in chat; a server can add its own, in any language. A spell's `gather_ticks` is how long it gathers in the palm, the Kidō key held, when cast by name. A Bakudō's `effect` takes `bound_seconds`, how long it binds the arms, and `held_seconds`, how long it holds a creature where it stands, each at most 60 and shortened against a stronger soul; a spell with both binds and holds at once. A spell's `look` may name how its bind is drawn on the creature with `bind` (`default`, `glow` or `rods`). A ward's `effect` takes `ward`: `seconds`, how long it stands, and for a smoke `lost_seconds`, `speed_seconds` and `speed_level`. A smoke reads `radius` as its cloud's and `blind_seconds` for the players in it; a shield reads `damage`, `damage_per_root` and `max_damage` as its strength and `angle` as the arc it covers. A wall reads `range` as how far before the caster it rises (1 to 8), `radius` as its half-width (up to 2.75) and `ward`'s `seconds` and `height` (up to 4). A heal reads `range` as its reach from the eyes and `radius` as its aim's margin, its cost, cooldown and xp are each second's, and it is never chanted or gathered; its tiers are Kubo's, in the code. Given an incantation, a shield cast by name could misfire for a quarter of its strength.
