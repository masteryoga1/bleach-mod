# Feature roadmap

This is the checklist the mod is built from. It covers what Bleach KD (1.12.2) had and what canon Bleach (the manga, the anime and Thousand-Year Blood War) has that KD left out, sorted into the rounds of the plan. Where KD and canon disagree, canon wins, and the "Canon notes" section at the end lists those disagreements.

Tick an item when it lands in a merged pull request. Reorder, cut or add items freely; nothing below is locked in.

**Tags**

- **[Canon+KD]**: in canon and in KD.
- **[Canon]**: in canon, missing from KD.
- **[KD]**: a KD invention, kept only because it fits canon and plays well.
- *(optional)*: low priority or off by default.

## Step 2: Core system

The foundations every path builds on: what a player's soul is, their spiritual energy, and how the game shows it.

- [x] **Soul state** [Canon+KD]: every player starts as a living, spiritually aware Human. Their data records a soul state (Human, Plus, Shinigami, Hollow, Arrancar) plus separate power tiers for Shinigami, Hollow, Quincy and Fullbring, so hybrids like Visored and Substitute Shinigami fit later.
- [x] **Spiritual awareness** [Canon+KD]: players below a reiryoku threshold cannot see or hit spirits. Hollows, Pluses and spirit-form players are hidden from them, with a faint shimmer and distant Hollow screams just below the threshold. *Hidden spirit players can still be heard, but Hollows and Pluses are heard only by those who can see them, and the crosshair passes through hidden spirits. Potions from the unaware pass through spirits, Instant Health and Harming included. Just below the threshold (two thirds of it by default) hidden spirits give off a faint shimmer and Hollow screams sound faint and far away; with the default starting reiryoku every player starts aware, so this matters only on servers that lower it.*
- [x] **Reiryoku and reiatsu** [Canon+KD]: one spiritual-energy pool that regenerates over time, faster in reishi-dense places and while resting. Running dry causes exhaustion (Slowness and Weakness) and ends any release. *Running dry ends the pressure aura and seals a Shikai. Reishi-dense places arrive with Soul Society.*
- [x] **Energy bar on the HUD** [Canon+KD]: a reiryoku bar beside the hotbar, plus slots for a chain timer, mask timer or Quincy reishi when those exist. Hidden for unaware Humans. *The chain gauge is in, and turns red during Encroachment. A Quincy gets a reishi gauge beside it, outlined blue for Blut Vene and red for Arterie, with Hollow poison filling it violet from the top and a draw bar above it. A sealed Quincy's gauge stays empty inside a grey outline. A thin art bar beside the gauge fills with the day's glove training (gold once the glove is mastered), the time left in Letzt Stil, the wait under the seal (white once the power can return), Vollständig (dimmed once its halo breaks) and its cooldown, and the reiryoku left while Ransōtengai is woven (pale blue). The draw bar turns orange when a Letzt Stil shot is charged, and also fills for the Licht Regen gather and the rite. A Visored gets a mask gauge in the chain's place: white with a red outline, emptying while the mask is on; grey, filling while it cannot be called again; bone when it is ready. During Shikai the bar is outlined in the blade's colour, and a gather bar fills above it while a Getsuga Tenshō gathers. A Hollow gets a soul hunger gauge in the chain's place (bone, amber when ravenous, a flashing red outline when starving) with a devour bar above it, and its food bar is hidden.*
- [x] **Zankensoki stats** [Canon]: Zanjutsu (swordplay), Hakuda (unarmed), Hohō (speed) and Kidō, plus Reiatsu Control, all levelled by use. Stats map onto vanilla attributes (attack damage, movement speed and so on) instead of KD's nine invented stats. *Kidō trains by casting spells: each cast gives the spell's own xp, and one more when it strikes, and a chanted cast twice as much; each second of Kaidō that heals, lifts Poison or Wither, or refills trains one; the Academy instructor's daily lesson gives a student 40.*
- [x] **Death into a Plus** [Canon+KD]: an aware Human who dies comes back as a Plus at the death point, with a Chain of Fate on their chest. *A death spot inside a wall, in a liquid, fire or something else harmful, outside the world border, over the void, or in the Dangai sends the Plus to their normal respawn point instead. A respawn anchor that was not needed gets its charge back. The chain is a placeholder built from vanilla models, and the Hollow mask is the mod's own placeholder model.*
- [x] **Chain of Fate and Encroachment** [Canon+KD]: the chain shortens over time, and near the end its links eat each other. A Konsō before it runs out saves the soul; if it runs out, the hole and mask form and the player becomes a Hollow. *The chain lasts an hour, wears twice as fast below 30% health and half as fast near a Shinigami (a player, a Substitute or an NPC), and dying as a Plus costs five minutes of it. It wears only in the Human World and the Dangai: in Soul Society, the Nether, the End and Hueco Mundo it holds, unless the Plus gnaws at it, and a Plus may stay there as long as it likes, its chain as it was (one in Encroachment stays in it, without its pangs, until it returns). The last quarter brings Encroachment pangs, and a chain that runs out makes the player a Hollow. A Konsō from a Shinigami frees the soul, and in the Overworld one on duty comes to a Plus who finds none (see Konsō). A Plus can also gnaw at its own chain with the Devour key, 40 times as fast.*
- [x] **Reiatsu difference** [Canon]: attacks from someone with far lower spiritual pressure do much less damage, like Kenpachi shrugging off weak blows. This replaces KD's full-armour damage shield. *Hits land fully up to a 3:1 gap, then shrink, never below a tenth. Hollows, Pluses and Shinigami NPCs have reiatsu of their own (a Hollow's grows with its size and with every Plus it devours); other creatures count their max health.*
- [x] **Reiatsu pressure aura** [Canon]: a toggle that floods the area with your reiatsu, slowing and frightening much weaker players and mobs while draining your energy. *On R by default. Releasing costs a second and a half's worth of reiryoku up front, then drains 5% of the maximum a second with no regeneration. After holding it in, your reiatsu takes two seconds to settle, with no regeneration and no new release. It reaches further as reiryoku grows, and creatures it weighs down hard stop attacking and run away (a hissing creeper's fuse dies down). It spares your mount, your pets and players you couldn't hurt anyway (PvP off, or a team without friendly fire).*
- [x] **Stats screen** [Canon+KD]: shows soul state, stats, reiryoku, zanpakutō name and release state, and what comes next on your path. *Soul state, reiryoku, the Chain of Fate, soul hunger and the souls devoured, whether reiatsu is released, the Hollows purified and slain and Pluses sent on, stats, awakened powers and the next step, plus whether the soul is a Substitute or an Academy student and, in Soul Society, for a Plus, a soul that may enrol and a student, which way the Academy lies (from outside Seireitei's wall, by its nearest gate), unless it is already there. Until the blade has a name it shows the Asauchi bond; after, the zanpakutō's name and whether it is sealed, in Shikai or always released. An Arrancar sees its own blade's name and whether it is in Resurrección, sealed or still waiting to be released, the form it rose from, how to call out the release, the keys for its arts and how much of an equal's blow its Hierro turns aside, its rank among the Arrancar, and what a Gran Rey Cero costs or what a seat among the Espada needs. A Gillian sees how to call down Negación. A Shinigami with a Hollow rooted in its wounds is told to give it a day and then face it in Jinzen, to rest after a loss, and when it is waiting. A Visored who has never worn its mask is told how to call it; a Visored's soul state says so, with its mask's time left, its wait or that it is ready, and how long its masks have been worn towards the masked Cero, or the Cero key once it has it. A Shinigami whose Hollow is not yet beaten is told that it may seize the body near death, and while it has, and, when a server lets lost fights make a Hollow, how many it has lost. A soul that may use Kidō has a Kidō button beside Done, which opens its Kidō page: the spells it knows down the left, the chosen one marked, and on the right the one under the mouse, else the chosen one: its kind and what its name means, the level to cast it by name, its cost and how long the hands stay busy, what it does at the soul's strength and (with chat casting on) the level to chant it and its incantation, or for Kaidō its reach, its cost a second, what the soul's tier heals and lifts and what the next tier brings. Under the list is the next spell to learn; above it, why the Kidō key would refuse now (a Hollow holding the body, bound arms, lent power, a level still to reach to cast the chosen spell by name, too little reiryoku). A click on a spell chooses it, as Sneak + U would.*

No overloaded vanilla keys. *So far: the stats screen on K, reiatsu release on R, Shunpo on V, Reishi Footing (unbound; jumping again while falling also works and can be turned off in the client config), zanpakutō Release on Z, Seal on B and a Technique key on G, Blut on N, Quincy Form on H (the Sanrei Glove, Letzt Stil, Licht Regen, Vollständig, Sklaverei and Ransōtengai), Devour on J (Sneak + J turns a Hollow's sight on and off), Cero on Y (a masked Visored's too) and a Visored's Hollow Mask on M (which also tears off the mask a Hollow forms when it seizes the body). ## Step 3: Shinigami path

The first playable slice: become a Shinigami, awaken your zanpakutō and hunt Hollows in the Overworld. The Academy is a placeholder building, which stands in Seireitei.

**Becoming a Shinigami**

- [x] **Academy instructor and Asauchi** [Canon+KD]: an instructor NPC in a small Academy building in Seireitei enrolls aware players and hands out an Asauchi. Graduation checks the four Zankensoki stats and the field trial. *So far: the Academy is a small building in Seireitei, 300 blocks south-west of its centre at (-192, 224), its gate facing east; it is built with the ground in a new world, and in one played before, the first time its ground is loaded or a soul is sent there, over whatever stands there. Academies that generated in the Overworld before keep their instructors, who send a Plus on to Soul Society and still enrol, teach, begin the field trial and graduate where they stand. The instructor cannot be hurt, pushed, pulled or led away. A Plus senses that it lies in Soul Society, and there which way, from outside Seireitei's wall by the nearest of its four gates. Only souls that have passed on can enroll: a Plus must pass on by Konsō first, and the living are warned away. The exams ask for level 5 Zanjutsu, Hakuda, Hohō and Kidō, and a passed field trial unless the server turns that off (see the Shin'ō Academy), and a student can cast Kidō from its first day, and chant Hadō #31 Shakkahō, the Academy's own spell, from Kidō 5. Once a day (a new day comes each morning, or every 20 minutes of play) the instructor gives a student a Kidō lesson: 40 Kidō xp, then the next spell its level will open, named in chat with its incantation (with chat casting on); the button greys out until the next day. A student whose Quincy power bars Kidō is told the instructor cannot teach Kidō to a Quincy, and a graduate is offered no lesson. Graduates get their first Shinigami tier, 25 reiryoku and a Shihakushō. The Asauchi never breaks.*
- [x] **Substitute Shinigami** [Canon]: Ichigo's route. A wounded Shinigami NPC, or another Shinigami player, transfers power to a living Human, who becomes a Substitute Shinigami and keeps their body. *A wounded Shinigami sometimes turns up at night near an aware Human with at least 60 reiryoku. A Shinigami player with at least 80 reiryoku can lend half of it for 20 minutes, during which they regenerate at half speed and cannot use Shunpo, Konsō, reishi footing or their release. Either way the Human gains 25 reiryoku, their first Shinigami tier and an Asauchi, and stays alive and visible. A Substitute who dies becomes a full Shinigami. The badge and leaving the body come later.*
- [x] **Asauchi bond** [Canon+KD]: carrying the Asauchi, meditating with it and killing Hollows with it builds a bond. At full bond the blade can awaken. *While a Shinigami, Substitute or Rukongai soul carries an unbound Asauchi, it bonds: 2 a minute for carrying it, 1 for each stretch of meditation, 25 for a Jinzen (once every 5 minutes), up to 50 for purifying a Hollow with it (half the Hollow's reiatsu), 15 for a close call (30 for a Substitute) and 30 for a full night's sleep (once every 20 minutes). The blade whispers at each quarter. A soul whose power is lent does not bond.*
- [x] **Jinzen and the inner world** [Canon]: sit in Jinzen (sneak and use with the blade across your lap) to meet your zanpakutō spirit. In this step it is a dialogue screen with a test; the real inner-world dimension comes later. *Sneak and hold use with the blade in hand, the off hand empty, on solid ground. After three still seconds a screen opens on the spirit's world. Below a quarter bond the blade is silent; below full the spirit stirs and shows what it is becoming. At full bond, with 100 reiryoku and Shinigami power, the spirit sets its test: picking out the red ribbon in time, answering its question, or striking through closing jaws. A failed test can be retried after 3 minutes, with more time for the ribbons. Moving, being hurt or closing the screen ends Jinzen; it counts as meditating while it lasts.*
- [x] **Zanpakutō spirit, name and release command** [Canon]: the spirit reveals its name and its release command (like "Scatter, Senbonzakura"). Learning the name turns the Asauchi into your own zanpakutō, bound to you. *Passing the test teaches the name and the command, grows reiryoku by 10, and binds the Asauchi to its owner: it takes the spirit's name and colour and answers to nobody else. A named soul's blade is kept through death, and a new Asauchi takes the spirit's form again if the old blade is lost.*
- [x] **Zanpakutō type** [Canon+KD]: a hidden affinity built up from how and where you fight while bonding with the Asauchi decides which kind of spirit you meet: power type, kidō type (elemental, poison, illusion) or constant release. *How the blade was used while bonding decides the spirit: purifying, mask strikes, Hakuda and Zanjutsu lean to power; Jinzen, reishi footing, Reiatsu Control, Kidō that strikes a Hollow and fighting in rain, water, snow or desert lean to kidō; released reiatsu, close calls and raw reiryoku without control lean to constant release, and Substitutes lean that way most. No kidō-type spirit ships yet, so a kidō leaning meets the next-closest kind.*
- [x] **Canon zanpakutō presets** [Canon]: spirits are defined in data. Step 3 ships **Zangetsu** and one simpler power type such as **Zabimaru**; more presets arrive in later rounds. *Spirits are datapack files (`data/<namespace>/bleach/zanpakuto_spirit/`), read when the world loads. Zangetsu (constant release, Getsuga Tenshō) and Zabimaru (power type, "Howl", a segmented whip-sword) ship now.*

**Shikai and combat**

- [x] **Shikai release** [Canon+KD]: press Release to say the command and name. The same blade changes form and gains its ability while reiryoku drains; it seals again when you choose or run dry. *Release on Z says the command and the name to aware souls nearby; the blade changes form (Zabimaru: 8 damage and 3 blocks more reach) and drains reiryoku with no regeneration (Zabimaru 0.6% of maximum a second, 2% less per Reiatsu Control level, down to 40%). It seals on B, when reiryoku runs out, after a second out of hand, when tossed, on sleep, death or logout, and when power is lent. Zangetsu is released once and never seals.*
- [x] **Getsuga Tenshō** [Canon+KD]: Zangetsu's signature move. Reiatsu gathered in the blade leaves the tip on the swing as a pale blue-white crescent that pierces several targets. Damage scales with reiatsu, and breaking blocks follows the mobGriefing rule. *Hold the Technique key with Zangetsu released to gather reiatsu (full in a second and a half) and let go to swing it out: 8 damage plus 0.8 per square root of maximum reiryoku, up to 60, scaled by the charge, piercing 3 to 5 targets and purifying Hollows. It costs 6% to 16% of maximum reiryoku with a 1-second cooldown. It is weaker until it has been used ten times and Zangetsu names it. It cuts soft blocks (hardness up to 0.8, no block entities) only where mob griefing, spawn protection and building rights allow. A masked Visored's is black with a red edge and does half again the damage (at most 108).*
- [x] **Shunpo** [Canon+KD]: a short flash-step dash whose distance grows with Hohō. No outfit needed. *On V, from Hohō 5: 6 blocks plus 0.2 per Hohō level, up to 16, for 5% of maximum reiryoku with a 1.5-second cooldown, falling to 2.5% and half a second at Hohō 50. On the ground it runs flat along WASD, climbing or dropping a block at a time; in the air it follows your gaze and ends on reishi footing. It stops at walls, lava, fire and the world border. Shinigami, Substitutes and Academy students can use it, and Slowness shortens it.*
- [x] **Konsō** [Canon+KD]: tap a Plus on the forehead with the pommel to send it on. This is the core early loop and grows reiatsu. *Sneak and use your Asauchi on a Plus player, who accepts by using an empty hand on you within 30 seconds. They become a Rukongai soul with no chain and, from any world but Soul Society (where Konsō moves no one), wake in a district of the Rukongai drawn by chance, the four regions alike and the inner districts likelier (half land in the first 20): it becomes their home, they are told its name and, if they can enrol, the way to the Academy. A server can send them straight to the Academy's gates instead, or nowhere. Wherever they are sent on, a bed or anchor they set outside Soul Society is let go. With no spawn of its own, a soul that has passed on wakes at its Rukongai home when it dies (one drawn then if it has none, or the Academy's gates where the server sends souls straight there), a student at the Academy and a Shinigami (a Substitute that has died once among them) at the Senkaimon court. The Shinigami gains 3 maximum reiryoku, for up to 10 Konsō a day. The instructor and the wounded Shinigami perform it too, and in the Overworld a Shinigami on duty comes to a Plus who finds none: once its chain has worn five minutes (or a quarter of it, if that is less), at any hour, one comes down on open ground 8 to 16 blocks away, or on a floor nearer by at the Plus's own height when the Plus is under a roof, in a cave or on a tower, and the Plus is told which way to look. It offers Konsō and returns to Soul Society three minutes later, or just after sending the Plus on. While the Plus refuses, another comes every ten minutes; none comes while a Shinigami NPC is within 128 blocks. A Plus none can reach, or one in another world but Soul Society, is told why (where its chain holds, that it holds), once every ten minutes. One comes too, on the same terms, to a soul that has passed on (a Rukongai soul or a student) and stands in the Overworld with no bed or anchor of its own there, whether it was left there or came by a gate (a Senkaimon, the Dangai or the Kōtotsu), and tells it that this world is no longer its own: if it is still within 32 blocks half a minute on, it is sent, in the motes of a passing, to where it would wake with no bed of its own, its Rukongai home (one drawn then if it has none) or the Academy's gates, alive and with everything it has, its bed or anchor elsewhere kept, set off any mount and woken, and told why; one that walks off, leaves the world or turns creative is let be until the next visit, and one held in a Negación light or a Caja is not taken out of it. ## Canon notes

Where KD differs from canon, and what this mod does instead:

- **Spellings**: Resurrección (not "Resurrecion"), Asauchi (not "Asuchi"), Quincy: Letzt Stil (not "Leztz Stil"), Quincy: Vollständig, Blut Vene and Blut Arterie, Hirenkyaku, Gintō, Sanrei Glove, Ginrei Kojaku, Licht Regen, Sklaverei, Ransōtengai.
- **Getsuga Tenshō** belongs to Zangetsu, not to a "lunar" element. It leaves the blade with the swing, pale blue-white in Shikai and black with a red edge in Bankai. The black one first comes from Ichigo's Hollow (Ch. 166) and lets the Hollow in (Ch. 211-213), so the mod draws a masked Visored's black with a red edge too, and makes it hit harder by its own call. Zangetsu gives it its name once it has been used.
- **Visored takeovers**: before its host beats it, canon's inner Hollow takes the body near death (Ch. 165-166, 216) and when its host reaches for the blade's power (Ch. 211-213, 218), and Ichigo tears the half-formed mask off to come back (Ch. 166). The chances, times and boosts are the mod's own, and so is the 5-second loss of control behind a beaten Hollow's mask, which canon never shows (there the black Getsuga only makes a takeover easier). Canon's Hollowfied Shinigami who lose become Hollows (Ch. -97, 219); here that is a server's choice.
- **Kidō** is a Shinigami art taught at the Academy, not a Hollow's or a Quincy's. Casting by name alone (Eishōhaki) weakens a spell (Official Character Book SOULs, p. 257), so a spell that has an incantation keeps only part of its strength by the Kidō key, more with skill. Shō, Byakurai and Tenran have no known incantation, so they lose nothing. Canon shows a spell bursting in its caster's face only when the chant is skipped, so only a cast by name misfires here. The incantations are romaji read from canon's Japanese and the mod's own English, not an official translation. Raikōhō's takes either reading of its 虚城, kojō or kyōjō, since no manga page was read to settle which one it prints. The Hadō strike as beams here, and Tenran as a widening gust. Canon has Tenran spun from a sheathed zanpakutō, with a hand as a stand-in, and Raikōhō gathered as an orb of yellow lightning above the palm. That a hand is weaker, that Tenran blinds, and how long the orb takes are the mod's. Sai binds the arms behind the back, as Rukia binds Ichigo's (Ch. 1), and canon's Hainawa is a crackling yellow rope thrown from the hand that ties an arm (manga) or the body (anime); here it holds a creature where it stands instead, so the first two binds do different things. Canon gives neither a time nor a measure of strength: the 3 seconds, the contest and the shrug below half are the mod's, after canon's rule that a stronger soul breaks a lesser bind. Geki's red, paralysing glow is the anime's; the manga shows only its chant (Ch. 21). Rikujōkōrō's six beams of light strike the waist (Ch. 142). Canon gives no time for either, so the 2 and 5 seconds, and the pin of a seized body, are the mod's, and their English chants are the mod's own. A Hollow killed by Kidō is slain, not purified: only the blade sends a soul on. As far as the mod's research found, Sekienton and Enkōsen are seen only in the anime: Ōmaeda's red smoke against a Hollow (around Ep. 312) and Kira's #39, a spinning disc before him (around Ep. 218). No chant, size, time or strength for either was found, so all of them are the mod's. Dankū is canon's chantless wall of light: Aizen blocks a #88 with it, and the barrier then shatters (Ch. -98); Byakuya calls it proof against any Hadō below #90 as Zommari's Amor strikes it again and again (Ch. 302); and in the anime Unohana stops a #88 with it unchanted (Ep. 325). As far as found, canon gives it no chant, size, time or strength, so the 5 by 4 wall, its 6 seconds, the weighing of other arts and what passes it are the mod's. No Hadō above #63 is cast in the mod yet, and every Hadō stops at it, even one a datapack numbers #90 or more. Kaidō has no number, name list or known incantation. Every Shinigami learns its basics at the Academy, and Kubo grades it in four levels, each rarer than the last: stopping bleeding, relieving pain, healing wounds and restoring organs (Q&A #854). The mod's four tiers follow them; the rates, caps and what each lifts are the mod's. Kubo also calls Kaidō a talent apart from skill at Kidō (Q&A #716); the mod keys it on the Kidō level instead. Healing restores reiatsu before the body (Ch. 382); Isane heals an Arrancar at Unohana's word (Ch. 299), so enemies are healed; Unohana heals her own wounds mid-fight (Ch. 526); and Hachigen cannot heal with an arm lost (Ch. 377), so bound arms end it.
